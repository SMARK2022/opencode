## Source intent
Two CI hardening changes on the SMARK fork:
1. `setup-bun` composite action's "Install dependencies" step gains an `env`
   block (`ELECTRON_SKIP_BINARY_DOWNLOAD`, `ELECTRON_SKIP_DOWNLOAD`,
   `PLAYWRIGHT_SKIP_BROWSER_DOWNLOAD`, `PUPPETEER_SKIP_DOWNLOAD`,
   `CYPRESS_INSTALL_BINARY=0`, `HUSKY=0`) — aligned with the Windows step in
   build-opencode.yml — so CI jobs skip prebuilt electron/playwright/
   puppeteer/cypress binary downloads and husky hook installation. upstream-e2e
   playwright browsers are installed by a dedicated test.yml step
   (step-level env does not persist across steps), and native postinstall
   (node-pty/tree-sitter) ignores these variables.
2. `ghostty-web` dep pinned from floating `github:anomalyco/ghostty-web#main`
   to immutable commit `513463a6f1190253057e8a3f0dac8f6ee8393553` in both
   `bun.lock` and `packages/app/package.json`, eliminating lockfile churn and
   redownloads when upstream main moves. The pin is the already-resolved
   commit — zero behavior change.
