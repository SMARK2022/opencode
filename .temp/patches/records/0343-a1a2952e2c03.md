### Hunk 1 — `@@ -2407,6 +2407,16 @@` (index.tsx, BlockTool new state)
The upstream commit adds three new reactive primitives to `BlockTool`:
- `hasPreview = createMemo(() => props.preview !== undefined)` — tracks whether a preview node exists
- `bodyMounted` signal + `createEffect` — body lazy-mounts on first expand (or immediately if no preview), then stays mounted permanently. This avoids paying diff/tree-sitter render cost on first paint while preventing the remount-after-destroy bug.

The core fix: OpenTUI's renderable cannot reliably remount the same JSX object after `<Show>` destroys it, causing blank content after collapse→expand→collapse.
